StreamLiveX is a bring-your-own IPTV player. Load your own IPTV subscription and watch it on phone, tablet, Android TV and Google TV in one clean, multi-language interface.

IMPORTANT: StreamLiveX does NOT provide or include any channels, movies or series. It is only a client that plays the M3U / Xtream Codes subscription YOU already own. Providing content legally is entirely the responsibility of the user's own service.

WHAT IT DOES
• Imports your M3U, Xtream Codes and EPG data.
• Brings live TV, movies and series into a single library.
• Uses TMDB posters and summaries for a rich, platform-like interface instead of a messy channel list.
• Full touch use on phone and tablet; full D-pad (remote) navigation on Android TV / Google TV.
• Plays HLS and MPEG-TS streams with the device's hardware codecs via Media3/ExoPlayer.

EXPERIENCE
• 6 languages: Turkish, English, Arabic, German, French, Spanish — full Arabic RTL support.
• Per-profile preferences and a kids profile.
• Category position memory and "Continue watching".
• When a stream fails, it clearly explains why (e.g. device codec / HEVC limit, offline source).

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Your IPTV username/password stays on your device.
• The app hosts no content; video traffic goes directly from your provider to your device.

An IPTV subscription is required. StreamLiveX does not sell subscriptions or content.
